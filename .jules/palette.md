## 2024-06-16 - Accessible Custom Filter Controls and Action Cards
**Learning:** In the Scout/Player dashboards, standard HTML elements (`input`, `select`) were used as custom filters without explicit `aria-label`s or associated `<label>`s, making their purpose ambiguous to screen reader users. Furthermore, making an entire div clickable (`onClick` on a container) with a visual, but disabled button (`pointerEvents: 'none'`) inside, completely breaks keyboard accessibility, as `div` elements do not receive tab focus and pseudo-buttons cannot be activated via keyboard.
**Action:** Always ensure filter controls (inputs, selects) have explicit, descriptive `aria-label` attributes if visible labels are not used. Always bind crucial actions (like selecting a card) to semantically interactive elements (like an actual `<button>`) to ensure keyboard navigateability and activation, rather than applying `onClick` handlers to non-interactive container `div`s.
