## 2026-06-03 - Form inputs lacking associated labels in Dashboard views
**Learning:** Filter controls (inputs and selects) in `PlayerDashboard.jsx` and `ScoutDashboard.jsx` often lack explicitly associated `<label>` elements or descriptive `aria-label` attributes, making them challenging for screen reader users to identify.
**Action:** Always ensure explicitly associated `<label>` elements or descriptive `aria-label` attributes are added to all inputs and selects in dashboard views.
