## 2026-05-27 - Accessible Dashboard Filters
**Learning:** Dashboard filter controls (inputs and selects) in the application's components like PlayerDashboard and ScoutDashboard often lack explicit accessible names. Without associated `<label>` elements or descriptive `aria-label` attributes, these inputs are not usable by screen readers.
**Action:** Always ensure interactive elements like filters and search inputs have explicitly associated `<label>` elements or descriptive `aria-label` attributes to support screen readers and meet accessibility standards.
