## 2026-05-26 - Accessible Names for Dashboard Filters
**Learning:** Dashboard filter controls (search inputs and selects) across different views often rely solely on visual placeholders or surrounding text context, completely lacking programmatic accessible names, making them difficult to understand for screen reader users.
**Action:** Always verify that input fields and selects have explicitly associated `<label>` elements or descriptive `aria-label` attributes to ensure they are fully accessible.
